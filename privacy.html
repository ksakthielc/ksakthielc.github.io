Privacy Policy

SCS PDF Unlocker & Scanner (Android app)

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Developer: SAKTHI CABI SRI (K. Sakthivel) · Contact: ksakthielc@gmail.com

In short

  • Your PDFs, photos and scans stay on your phone. We never upload them and we have no servers that receive them.
  • We do not ask for an account, your name, phone number or email.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads (details below).

1. Files you open and create

The app works on your device. It only reads the files you choose yourself (for example a PDF from Downloads or WhatsApp, or photos from your gallery).

  • All tools (scan, remove password, add password, join, take out pages, reduce size, arrange pages) run on your phone without internet.
  • Files you save are written to your phone's Downloads › SCS PDF folder, or to the place you choose. Files you share go only to the app you pick.
  • Passwords you type are used only to open or lock that file. They are not stored and not sent anywhere.
  • Removing a password works only when you already know that password. The app cannot break or guess passwords.

2. Camera

The app asks for camera permission only when you tap Camera to scan a page. Photos are processed on your phone and become part of the PDF you save. They are not uploaded. You can refuse or turn off the permission at any time in Settings › Apps › SCS PDF › Permissions; you can still use photos from your gallery.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • the device's Advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location based on it,
  • app usage and ad interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped),
  • device and crash/performance information.

This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

4. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information or file content. The app has no sign-in and no analytics of its own. Apart from the ad data described above, nothing leaves your device.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Because we do not receive your data, there is nothing for us to keep or delete on our side. Files you saved remain in your phone's storage until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its settings and temporary files. For ad data, use the Google controls listed in section 3.

6. Children

The app is a general tool for working with documents and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
